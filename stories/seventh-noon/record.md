# The Seventh Noon: the record (the author only; never given to any model)

What happened, as present facts with their chapter, so a Back can undo them. Under about 2,000 words.

## Now
- Day 8 (Tuesday), a little after one in the afternoon. Evan on the roof of Riverside Self Storage; Ava Torres in the
  street outside his gate, hands up, alone. (ch1)

## What happened
- ch1: the loading yard behind Harlan Paper Supply: three fed turned killed; a small blue core (three in his pocket now).
  The seventh noon on the roof: the ten names. Shouts and a long burst of gunfire to the north; the bridge's horn three
  times. Ava at the gate, saying his name.

## Who knows what
- Ava knows his name, his face, his base. Nobody else does (that he knows).

## People
- Ava Torres: the tenth name; at his gate. (ch1)
