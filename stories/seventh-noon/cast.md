# The cast sheet (the writer reads the sections a chapter's plan names on its CAST line; the hero always)
# Look, voice, what each one knows, wants and hides. Present facts; no lines of dialogue. Never given to the reader.

## Evan Cross
Evan Cross (he), the hero, 24. Look: tall and lean, dark hair cut short, a thin scar through his left eyebrow, gray
eyes; a black motorcycle courier jacket with the company name half peeled off (a piece torn from the shoulder in the
loading yard), cargo pants, work boots. Before: a courier for eight years who knew every street in Sable; five foster
homes; nobody waits for him and he waits for nobody. Voice: quick, dry, a little rude, funny mostly in his own head; he
talks to himself out loud when it is too quiet; casual and short with people, never speeches. Who he is: practical,
decides fast, does not look back, likes being good at things. He wants to get stronger before anyone else, because he
has seen what the strong are already doing to the weak, and since the seventh noon he knows he is a prize.
His power, exactly: THE HERO SHEET.
What he knows: only what the chapters show.

## Ava Torres
Ava Torres (she), 23, the tenth name. Look: medium height, strong shoulders and climber's hands (calluses, short nails,
taped fingers), dark hair tied back tight, brown eyes that do not blink much, a small silver ring in one ear; a black
rain jacket, jeans, running shoes, a backpack; a pistol in the back of her belt, under the jacket. Before: a
climbing-gym instructor. Voice: nothing like his. Earnest and direct, no jokes; she says the hard thing plainly and then
waits. When she is scared she talks fast and too much, then stops herself mid-sentence. When she is angry about
something big, it comes out at something small. She bargains badly when she is scared, and
knows it.
Her power, Sight: she sees the power in living things as light, its color and how much of it; the brighter a light, the
farther she sees it (a weak one through a wall or a floor, a strong one across the river at night, the brightest even
through the ground); she sees a core inside one of the turned before it drops. Since
the seventh noon she also sees a thin ring of white light around the head of each of the ten; each ring came on at the
moment its name was read. Sight shows power, never what anyone means to do. She fights with the pistol and a knife.
What she knows: she watched the brightest light by the river for three days from the Linden Towers across the water
(him, hunting the turned, never people); at noon a white ring came on around his head as the seventh name was read, so
she knew his name. Minutes after her own name was read (the last), Cal Brooks and two others came for her with guns;
Danny Reyes, nineteen, held the window and the fire stairs for her, and she ran across the river by the rail bridge.
What she wants: to live, and to be strong enough that nobody can do that to her again. Strong means kills of her own:
she wants to climb, not to be carried. Her offer is a deal: her eyes, and what she saw in three days of watching (where
the strong lights live), for his walls and his strength; her terms, when it comes to terms, are half of the cores,
never left behind, and Danny out. She will not beg.
What she hides, and lies about plainly if asked: that Cal's group is following her (she saw their lights on the rail
bridge behind her); that she wants Diego Santos dead (her mother was on the bus he burned on Fifth Street).

## Cal Brooks
Cal Brooks (he), 27, the leader of the Linden Towers group. Look: thick-necked, a gym body gone tired, blond buzz cut, a
gray hoodie, a hunting rifle he holds like he only learned it this week. Voice: loud when he is scared,
reasonable-sounding, a man who tells himself he is the good guy. Power: Strength, small (he can tear a car door off).
What he knows: Ava is the tenth name; she sees things; she ran across the river. He does not know who Evan is. What he
wants: Ava's power for himself, before anyone else takes it; he is afraid of what happens to him and his people without
it, and of Danny, who let her go.
With him: Ryan (his cousin, loyal, big, the shotgun); Kenji and Mara (scared, eighteen and twenty, pistols, following
whoever is loudest); Danny Reyes (nineteen, skinny, no gun, who held the window for Ava; Cal brought him because Ava
might come out for him, and he came because Cal's people hold his sister in the towers; he wants Ava alive and will not
leave without Kenji and Mara, his friends).
