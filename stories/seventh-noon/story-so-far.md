# What the player has seen, in short (the writer and the reader both get it; no secret ever goes here). Under 400
# words; rewritten as the story moves.

Sable, a big American city on a gray bay, in early summer. At noon on a Tuesday, every living person heard a calm voice
inside their head: every living thing now carries a power, most cannot hold it, and a kill wakes yours; it would speak
again at the seventh noon. About three people in four turned: alive, fast in short rushes, strong, always hungry; a bite
does not turn anyone, and the ones that eat well grow bigger and smarter about doors. Animals woke too, and some grew.
Kills make the living stronger; a strong kill sometimes leaves a core, a small crystal the color of what it carried,
which makes you stronger when you crush it. Phones died on day two, the power on day one. Guns work.

Evan Cross, a motorcycle courier with nobody, spent the first week hunting the turned alone from his base in Riverside
Self Storage by the river. His first kill, his manager, woke Lightning; an owl gave him Night Eyes; three hours of
fighting woke Tireless. Nine cores crushed.

The city a week in, as he has seen it: silent streets, crows and flies; smoke from fires in the east; gunfire most nights
from the north, near the National Guard armory; on the Harbor Bridge to the south, somebody with many people and guns
holds both ends and shoots anyone who comes close; a church bell at dusk in the old town. He has seen a woman jump roof
to roof, and a man on Fifth Street burn a bus full of people with his bare hands.

The seventh noon (day eight, a Tuesday): the voice named the ten strongest in the city: Victor Crane, Mei Lin Zhou,
Diego Santos, Hannah Volk, Tobias Grant, Daniel Okoye, Evan Cross, Samuel Price, Nadia Petrova, Ava Torres. "Kill one of
them, and what they carry is yours." It will speak again at the thirtieth noon. Right after, shouting and a long burst
of gunfire far to the north, then the bridge's horn three times. An hour later a woman stood at his gate with her hands
up and said his name out loud: "My name's Ava Torres. You heard it an hour ago."
