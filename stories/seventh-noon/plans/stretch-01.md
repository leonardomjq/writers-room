# Stretch 1: The Tenth Name (chapters 2 to about 4; day 8, from one in the afternoon) (the author only)

## What it is about
The first hours after the naming. The list turns everyone into a hunter, and the first hunters are not monsters: they
are scared young people who were Ava's friends this morning, split among themselves over who gets her power. Evan learns
what a named one is worth, what Sight is, and what it means that one pair of eyes in the city can see every ring.

## The rule of this stretch: his choices change the story
Each pick changes who holds Sight, who has seen his face, and who is his enemy; the world's clock (Cal's group half an
hour behind her, Diego's riders on day 9, Mei Lin's chalk on day 10) keeps moving, but what reaches him, and why,
depends on what he did. A refusal is never erased by a fight forced on him.
- **He talks to her (pick 1):** chapter 2 is the talk, with no crossroads at its end: she proves her eyes inside his
  own walls, admits the five on her trail, and puts her terms down. It ends on Cal's five turning into his street,
  with the answer still his to type. Chapter 3 brings them to the gate, and the crossroads comes there, with the guns
  in sight: take her deal (four guns at his fence, and a partner who wants kills of her own), kill her and take her
  eyes, or send her out the gate.
- **He kills her (pick 2):** it is an attempt, rolled from what he does and what she has. If it works he takes Sight
  raw (a flood of light he cannot read) and her ring, as Cal's five come up the street toward her body; that plays on
  to its end without another crossroads.
- **He drives her off (pick 3):** she gets away on her own, over the roofs. Cal's five stop at his gate and talk to a
  stranger; he stays out of her war, and the cost is that the one pair of eyes that can find every named one is out
  there with his name.

## After the second crossroads (forecast, bent by what he picks)
- A fight against people with guns is a different fight from the turned in the loading yard of chapter 1: guns that
  kill in one lucky shot (a gun kill counts), loyalties that change in the middle of it, and witnesses who live. Cal's group is not one mind: Danny wants Ava alive; the two
  scared kids follow whoever is loudest; Ryan follows Cal. A side that breaks, or an attacker who sees the lightning,
  refuses the fight and leaves to sell what he saw, is more troubling than a clean win.
- No level for killing Cal: his power is small. Lightning 5 waits for a fight that should have killed Evan.
- Whatever happens, by day 9 someone knows something about him they did not know at noon: Cal's survivors (a lightning
  man at Riverside Storage, with or without the tenth name), or nobody, if he stayed out. On day 9 Diego's riders burn
  the Linden Towers; what the towers' survivors sell to save themselves depends on what they saw.
- If Ava lives and is with him: the night on the roof, the city through her eyes, the rings she can read from there
  (two rings together in the north: a named one has killed a named one; who and why, nobody here knows yet). It must change a
  practical assumption, not only confirm the rule: she also sees a ring where none should be (forecast: Tobias's ring
  moving through the streets at night, three streets from the lit hospital, alone).

## The rhythm
One big call starts a run of consequences. Chapter 1 ended on a crossroads, so chapter 2 never does. Pick 1 earns its
second call at chapter 3; picks 2 and 3 play on without one until the end of the stretch (Diego coming, chapter 4 or 5).
A refusal is never punished by the same trap twice.

## Plants (for later; never pointed at)
- The boy who held the window (Danny): a person who chose a side once and may again.
- One good shot: guns are the great equalizer.
- Her map with marks (pick 2): the places she saw strong lights.
- His face: he believes nobody can put it to his name; eight years of deliveries say otherwise (stretch 2 or 3).

## The twist
Ava's lie is small and human (she had nowhere else). The bigger turn is that the rule works on the people closest to
you: her own group came for her minutes after her name, and Victor shot his own man. Nobody is safe from the people
around them, which is the question his whole story will ask of the people he gathers.
