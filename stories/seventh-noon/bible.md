# The Seventh Noon: the bible (the author only; never given to the writer or the reader, never quoted or hinted to the player)

The truth of the world, the ten, the clocks and the forecast. The writer gets slices of it through a chapter's plan and
the cast sheet (cast.md), never this file. Kept short: a person who has not come to matter stays a line.

## 1. What the world is
- **Power is one thing, and it is conserved.** On the first noon every living thing got a share. Most bodies could not
  hold theirs: they turned. The turned still carry it, which is why killing them pays; eating passes it too, which is
  why the fed ones grow (the turned eat each other and the dead, and so do woken beasts). A kill takes a part of what the
  dead carried; a core is a lump of it left behind by a strong kill. Over the weeks the share of the city flows into
  fewer and stronger bodies, human and not. What neither the killer nor a core takes soaks into the living things
  nearest the death (a street where many died grows big rats, big dogs, fed turned). Nobody knows this yet; it is learned
  in pieces (the fed growing, the ten, the big eaters), never explained by anyone.
- **The voice** is the Tally: whatever it is, it counts and it speaks at the noons it names, calm, never cruel, never
  kind. It does not answer questions. It speaks to one person alone when their power grows or something new wakes in
  them, in short bracketed lines.
- **The ten and the mark.** At the seventh noon the voice marked the ten *people* who carried the most power, each ring
  coming on at the moment its name was read. It counts only people: the strongest things in Sable (the Canal Street
  eater, the oldest of Hannah's dogs) are on no list, and nobody knows that. Whoever kills one
  takes everything the dead carried (every skill, at its level) and the mark. The mark is invisible, except to Ava's
  Sight: a thin ring of white light around the head. A person with two marks wears two rings. At the thirtieth noon the
  voice names the ten again, from who carries the most then: a killer of a named one is near certain to be named. The
  ring passes with the mark: an unnamed killer wears the dead's ring; a named killer wears two. Anyone who takes Ava's
  Sight can see every ring in the city. A ring shows a mark, never how it came there: two rings say a named one killed
  a named one, nothing about who or why.
- **The Gray** (forecast, not yet seen by him): the city is closed. The highways out end in a slow wall of gray fog
  past the suburbs; whoever walks into it walks out again facing the city, lost for hours. Planes stopped on day one; no
  radio from outside. The bridge people know (they tried the far shore); most do not.
- **Guns work, and a gun kill counts.** A frightened man with a rifle can take a named one's power. This is why faces
  matter: almost nobody knows what the ten look like.

## 2. Evan's power (the rules I keep; what he sees is in hero.md)
- Kills of things that carry power make his skills grow; a level comes after a real fight with a strong kill, at most
  one a chapter, with the voice's line word for word. Levels 1 to 5 are unlocks (new things he can do, never numbers).
  At 5 a skill can evolve into a bigger form; the voice names three deeds when 5 arrives.
- **Lightning:** (the evolution's three deeds stay, but chasing one should cost him another interest; never three
  convenient trials in a row) 1 spark on contact; 2 a strike that stops a heart; 3 a bolt thrown about five steps; 4 the bolt to about
  ten steps, and bursts of speed. **5 (next):** the bolt jumps from the first thing it hits to two more near it; and he
  can hold a charge in his hand for about a minute and let it go at once, a blast that throws a man across a room.
  **The evolution (at 5, the voice's three deeds):** be struck by lightning from the sky and live; kill one of the ten
  with lightning; crush a core that is white. It becomes **Storm**: he calls lightning down from the sky itself.
- **Night Eyes** (from the owl, day three): he sees in the dark as if it were evening. Grows only by deeds (forecast: at
  the next level he sees the heat of bodies through thin walls).
- **Tireless** (day five, three hours of fighting): he tires much slower. Next level after another fight past what a body
  can take: his wounds close faster.
- New skills: rare and clear, from a strong kill that carried a trait (a beast, a named one), a deed, or a habit kept up.
  A named one's kill gives all their skills at once.
- Cost: heavy use leaves him shaking and starving; a big push past that makes him black out.
- Cores: he has crushed nine. Three small blue ones in his pocket now. A small core is a small push; a big one, after a
  strong kill, is a real step. A core is the color of what the dead carried (blue: lightning-like, or cold; red: heat).

## 3. The ten (as named at the seventh noon), each already moving
1. **Victor Crane**, 32. Ex-army sergeant, then a rich family's head of security. Big, shaved head, a calm low voice,
   polite until he is not. **Iron** (his skin turns dark gray metal: bullets flatten on him; his strength) and, since an
   hour after the naming, **Mend** (wounds close as he watches), taken from Samuel Price. Holds the National Guard armory
   on Fort Street in the north with about forty men, guns, trucks, fuel and its own well. At night he lights the walls
   and the turned come to the noise; his men shoot them from the roof and grow (the gunfire every night). He holds his
   men with the ammunition, the well and Mend (he is close to unkillable); after Samuel he told them his rule: a name is
   brought to him alive, and he says who kills it; a man who kills a name on his own is shot by the others, and Victor
   takes what he carried. The flaw his men can see: a man who grows strong enough on the nightly hunts may not need
   Victor. Alvarez, a shooter on the roof, has been growing faster than the rest and hiding it (he misses strong
   targets in front of others and hunts alone at dawn); Victor's own system is making his rival. Want: Sable, whole. Plan: bind or kill the names, then take the bridge's
   food. Wears two rings.
2. **Mei Lin Zhou**, 27. A firefighter. Short, strong, hair cut to the jaw, a burn scar on one hand; blunt and tired.
   **Wind**: she leaps roof to roof (the woman Evan saw), moves in gusts, and cuts with a blade of pressed air. Holds the
   old town around St. Agnes church, four blocks barricaded, about three hundred people; the bell at dusk calls survivors
   in. Want: her people alive. Plan: a pact of the named against Victor; her runners chalk an invitation on walls across
   the city. Her answer to protection is walls and rules: she takes in anyone, and puts a thief out into the street at
   night, which is a death; once she broke that rule, for a thief who is the only one who can keep the rain tanks and the
   pump working, and her people know it and have not forgiven it. She pulled Nadia Petrova out of a burning club on day two. She will kill to protect her
   people and does not pretend otherwise.
3. **Diego Santos**, 22. A small-time dealer, handsome, loud, laughs at his own cruelty. **Fire**: from his hands, a
   wall of it, and he can walk through it. The man who burned the bus on Fifth Street. Holds the east side (the three
   smoke columns: he burns blocks to drive people out to him) from Eastgate Mall, about twenty-five young men and women
   who follow him for food and fear. Under the laughing he is terrified of the turned: on the first noon one ate his
   mother in front of him, and he cannot stand to be near one. He burns blocks to keep them away from him, which is why
   the east burns; up close, with no fire between him and one of them, he freezes. Want: everything, and fun; under it,
   never to be that close again. Plan: hunt the lower names for their power, west across the river, burning as he goes.
4. **Hannah Volk**, 30. A school librarian, quiet, who never raised her voice. Tall, wiry, hair gone gray in a week. **Beast**: a woken wolf's strength,
   smell and speed, claws when she wants them; a pack of a dozen woken dogs, grown huge, follow her. Holds Kessler Park
   and the zoo. Her power is eating her mind: each day less of her talks. Every morning she writes her own name on the
   wall of the zoo office, over and over, to keep it; the dogs obey her only while she still can. Want: nothing from
   people. A lethal place, not a rival; her pack ranges further each night.
5. **Tobias Grant**, 19. A first-year student, thin, glasses, terrified. **Stone**: walls rise from the ground, stone
   skin. His father, Dr. Alan Grant, runs the survivors in Sable General Hospital (medicine; the generators still run:
   one of the few lit buildings) and uses Tobias as its wall. Tobias wants out and hates killing; at night he slips out
   with stolen medicine for a family his father turned away, three streets over. His father wants to sell the hospital
   and his son's protection to the strongest (he will deal with Victor).
6. **Daniel Okoye**, 29. A city bus driver, big, slow to speak, kind and stubborn. **Frost**: cold from his hands, ice
   that spreads, shards. Leads about thirty ordinary survivors in Lincoln High. His answer to protection is the opposite
   of Mei Lin's: he puts nobody out and will not fight people. He is stubborn and resentful, not soft: on the first day
   he killed his neighbor, turned, with a car jack, and has not hit anyone since. The bridge stopped trading with him
   when he refused to hand over a boy they said stole from them, and his school is starving because of it. Want: keep them
   fed; he will look for a protector or a pact, and he is the named one most likely to trust the wrong person.
7. **Evan Cross**, the hero.
8. **Samuel Price**, dead. One of Victor's own lieutenants; minutes after the names were read, Victor emptied a rifle
   into him on the armory yard (the shouting and the long burst in the north). Victor now carries his Mend.
9. **Nadia Petrova**, 25. A bartender from the Lantern Mile clubs, Russian-born, sharp, funny, dangerous. **Venom**: her
   nails grow hard and long, and a scratch numbs, then stops a heart. Lives on the top floors of the Grand Harbor Hotel
   downtown with five women she took in; hunts the hunters who come for her, never anyone else. She owes Mei Lin her
   life and hates owing. Want: climb, and never be anyone's again. Plan: kill a higher name; she looks for one she can
   get close to, and Mei Lin's meeting would put several in one room.
10. **Ava Torres**, 23. See section 4.

## 4. Ava Torres (the tenth name, at his gate)
- Look and voice in cast.md. A climbing-gym instructor before. Her voice is not his: earnest and direct, no jokes;
  when she is scared she talks fast and too much, then stops herself mid-sentence; she gets angry at the wrong small
  thing (being called lady, a door left open) instead of the big one.
- **Sight**: she sees the power in living things as light, its color and how much; the brighter the light, the farther
  she sees it (a weak one through a wall or a floor, a strong one across the river at night, the brightest even through
  the ground); she sees cores inside the bodies of the turned before they
  drop. She has hunted with it: the weak and fat ones that carry the most, which is why she is on the list. She fights
  with a pistol and a knife. Since the seventh noon she sees the ten's rings. She had watched the brightest light by the
  river for three days (him) from the Linden Towers across the water; at noon a ring of white came on around his head
  at the moment the seventh name was read. That is how she knew. Sight shows power, never what anyone means to do.
  Raw, it floods the eyes; she needed days to read it, and whoever takes it from her starts from nothing.
- **What she wants:** to live, and to be strong enough that nobody can do to her what her own people tried. Strong
  means kills of her own: she wants to climb, not to be carried, so a partner is also someone she will compete with for
  every kill and every core. Her terms, if it comes to terms: half of the cores, never left behind, and Danny out. Minutes
  after her name was read (the last), Cal Brooks and two others came for her with guns; Danny Reyes, nineteen, held the
  window and the fire stairs for her, and she ran. She crossed the river by the rail bridge and came straight to the one
  named she had watched kill only the turned. Her offer is a deal: her eyes and what she saw in three days of watching
  (where the strong lights live) for his walls and his strength. She bargains badly when she is scared, and knows it.
- **What she hides:** that Cal's group is following her (she saw their lights on the rail bridge behind her and came
  anyway, because there was nowhere else); that she wants Diego Santos dead (her mother was on the bus on Fifth Street;
  she has told nobody). She lies plainly and only about these. Forecast: at the moment Evan could deal with Diego (a
  truce against Victor, a trade), she will choose Diego's death over Evan's interest.

## 5. Others who matter
- **Cal Brooks**, 27, a personal trainer, the leader of the Linden Towers group (eight people, five guns: pistols, a
  hunting rifle, a shotgun). **Strength** (small: he lifts a car door off its hinges). Afraid, proud, and the voice made
  Ava the most valuable thing in his life. He wants her power for himself; the group is not one mind about it. With him
  on her trail, half an hour behind her: his cousin Ryan (loyal, the shotgun), two scared kids who follow whoever is
  loudest (Kenji and Mara), and Danny Reyes. Cal brought Danny because Ava might come out for him, and Danny came because
  Cal's people hold his sister Sofia in the towers; Danny wants Ava alive and will not leave without Kenji and Mara, who
  are his friends. The other three stayed in the towers with Sofia.
- **The bridge**: Marcus Hale, 36, a crane operator and union steward; about a hundred and twenty people on the Harbor
  Bridge and the container port at its south end (the food: shipping containers of canned goods and rice). Organized,
  armed, fair to their own, cold to everyone else. After the naming he blew the horn three times: hunting teams out.
  His market: food for news of the ten, posted on boards at the bridge's north end. He knows the city is closed.
- **The big eaters**: in the subway under Canal Street and the riverfront, the turned have been eating each other in the
  dark; one has eaten hundreds and is the size of a truck, and it carries more than any of the ten, with no ring. Ava's
  Sight can catch it deep under the riverfront streets as something brighter than anything she has seen. It comes up at
  night when the tunnels run empty (forecast: day 14 or so), and the turned keep disrupting human plans before then:
  routes closed by feeding grounds, a block taken by a pack of grown dogs.

- **Powers are random**: what someone did before says nothing about what woke in
  them; the librarian is the wolf. A weaker person who holds the right thing (a gun, a door, a well) often controls a
  scene the stronger one cannot afford to fight through.
- **Not every group is secretly ugly**: one of them (forecast: Daniel's school) keeps a hard promise at a cost, and is
  still Evan's opponent over something real.
- **Ordinary people** (the color of every street): a family living in a car wash who will not open the door to
  anyone; a man who walks Canal Street at dusk calling his wife's name; people drinking from the river and getting
  sick. Terror for weeks, bad choices, kindness that costs. Most people have no angle.
- **Water** is the scarce thing that makes kingdoms (never a chore for him): the taps died on day four with the pumping
  stations. The armory has its own well, the bridge has the port's bottled water, St. Agnes catches rain in the
  churchyard; everyone else drinks the river or the stores' last bottles. A group that holds water holds people.
- **His face** (planned, stretch 2 or 3): he is sure nobody can put his face to his name. He is wrong: eight years of
  deliveries. Someone who signed for his packages every week (a woman at the bridge's market who ran a bakery on Ninth)
  knows the courier's name and face, and has heard the list.

## 6. Places
Riverside Self Storage (his: four floors, orange roll-up doors, two stairwells, dead elevators, the roof; unit 418; the
basement held three turned, found and killed in chapter 2 if he talked to Ava). The warehouse district by the Sable River; across the river the Linden
Towers (apartments). Fort Street armory (north). Old town and St. Agnes (the bell). The Harbor Bridge and the port
(south). Eastgate Mall and the burning east. Kessler Park and the zoo. Sable General Hospital (lit). Lincoln High. The
Grand Harbor Hotel and the Lantern Mile (downtown). Canal Street and the subway under it.

## 7. Clocks (story days: the first noon is day 1, a Tuesday; the seventh noon came seven days later, on day 8, a
## Tuesday; the thirtieth noon comes thirty days after the first, on day 31, a Thursday)
- Day 8, about half an hour after Ava reaches his gate: Cal's group comes up the street on her trail, in daylight.
- Day 9: the bridge posts its first "food for names" board. Diego's riders cross the river at night, burning.
- Day 10: Mei Lin's chalk across the city: "The named: St. Agnes, Saturday at noon. Come in peace. Leave the others at
  home." Victor's trucks sweep the north for survivors to take in or strip.
- Day 11: Dr. Grant sends word to Victor. Daniel's school runs out of food.
- Day 12 (Saturday): the meeting at St. Agnes; who comes depends on what has happened (forecast: Mei Lin, Daniel,
  Tobias, Nadia; Victor sends a man; Diego comes to burn it).
- Day 13: Victor's men take the hospital "under protection". Day 14: the Canal Street eater comes up.
- Day 15 on: Victor moves on the bridge. Hannah's pack hunts the streets near the park at night.
- An event beyond his reach, with consequences he cannot reverse, now and then (forecast: day 10, the Lincoln High
  school's food runs out and half of Daniel's people walk to the bridge to beg; the bridge takes the young ones).
- Every time the voice speaks to the city it changes the world, never only a new list.
- **Day 31, the thirtieth noon:** the voice names the ten again (forecast: [The first month is over.] the new list, and
  [The way out opens for those who are named.]: the Gray opens a road for the ten alone, the start of the next act:
  other cities, their own ten). A forecast, never a script.

## 8. The course (forecast)
Act one, the first month: the scramble after the naming. Evan from a farmer to a man the city knows by his work, never
by his face; a base and a few people of his own; rivals by reputation before he meets them; the danger shifts from the
turned to people. Its spine: the race for the names and Victor's grip on the city; its end, the thirtieth noon.
