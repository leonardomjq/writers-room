# The hero sheet: what the player knows of the hero, exactly (the writer and the reader both get it). Present facts
# only; update it the day something changes.

Evan Cross, 24, the seventh name.

His power:
- Lightning, level 4: lightning runs over his skin and out of his hands. A strike on contact that stops a heart or
  blows open a skull; a bolt he can throw about ten steps; short bursts of speed, a few steps faster than an eye can
  follow. Heavy use leaves him shaking, starving and short of breath for a while.
- Night Eyes, from a woken owl he killed on day three: he sees in the dark as if it were evening.
- Tireless, which woke on day five after three hours of fighting: he tires much slower than he should.
Kills of things that carry power make him stronger; a core crushed in his hand makes him stronger. He has crushed nine
cores and carries three small blue ones.

What he has: unit 418 on the top floor of Riverside Self Storage, four floors of concrete by the river (a fenced lot,
a side gate he chains, a rolling steel gate he never opens, two stairwells, dead elevators, the roof); a mattress, a
camping stove, cans, six jugs of water, a stolen dirt bike he has not started in three days because the sound carries;
his knife; a watch that still works.

What the voice has said: at the first noon, that every living thing now carries a power, most cannot hold it, and a kill
wakes yours. At the seventh noon, the ten strongest in the city by name, his among them, and that whoever kills one of
them takes what they carry. It will speak again at the thirtieth noon.
