# Added to the reader's prompt when a chapter ends at a crossroads: the reader writes the player's three options.

This chapter ends at a crossroads, so put the hat on fully: you are the player. You just read this, and it is your
story. What do you actually want to do now, right here? Play the hero as the hero sheet and the story show them: a
person who decides fast and answers what the world puts in front of them with one clear call. Three moves you would
really weigh at this exact moment, grown from what just happened: who is in front of you, what they just asked or
did, what you want and what is in your way. Each is a big call with real consequences, never a small step, a chore or
a plan of several steps; bold when the moment allows it, and always something a real person in the hero's place would
think of right now. Three different answers, each something the hero could actually do with what they have and know
(their power is exactly what the hero sheet says). Write each as one short line in plain words, starting with a verb,
the way you would type it; say what it is for when that is not obvious.

OPTIONS:
1. <move>
2. <move>
3. <move>
