# Example chapters

Two chapters, not edited by hand, so you can see what you get before you set anything up. Each one went through the
full flow: the author planned it, one model wrote it, a fresh model read it as the player, the writer mended the
marked lines, and the author checked it.

- `small-change-ch01-claude.md`: chapter 1 of a new story the author built from one line ("a city where memories are
  sold as currency, and I'm a new thief who can steal them"). Written by Claude, read by Codex.
- `seventh-noon-ch02-codex.md`: chapter 2 of the demo, after the player picked "Drive her off with a threat" and then
  asked for a rewrite with the note "make Evan colder, fewer words". Everything on Codex. Chapter 1 is in
  `stories/seventh-noon/chapters/ch01.md`.

Claude's prose is the stronger of the two; Codex's is more plain.

[`what-the-room-catches.md`](what-the-room-catches.md) shows what the reader marked on a real draft, and what changed.
