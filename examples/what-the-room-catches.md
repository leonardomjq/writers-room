# What the room catches

From one real run: chapter 2 of the demo, after the player picked "Go down and talk to her through the fence". Written and read by Claude. Nothing here was edited by hand. Spoilers for that road.

The reader is a fresh model that knows only what the player knows. It marks every place it would stop reading, and says why.

**The draft:** "On the fifth day two people went past you on Canal Street with a cart."

**The reader:** She only sees light in living things, and a cart has no light. She was also across the river, so how does she know?

**The final:** "On the fifth day two weak lights went right past you on Canal Street."

---

**The draft:** "Your jacket sits wrong in the back," Evan said.

**The reader:** She is facing him and wearing a backpack, so he can't see the back of her jacket.

**The final:** "Your right hand keeps wanting to go behind your back," Evan said.

---

**The reader on the whole chapter:**

> Mostly yes. Ava's plan, her lie and the way it breaks all feel real, and I would have checked the basement too. Three things don't add up: she reads things that have no light, like a cart. Evan spots a pistol on a person who is facing him. The clock is off. The five were already behind her at the bridge, yet after the talk, the fight and the knife work they are still "about ten minutes away".

The writer then fixes those lines with small edits, and every other word stays. After that the author, who knows the secrets, checks the final against the bible and every chapter before.
